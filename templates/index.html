How ResolveDesk Works

A step-by-step transparent pipeline from grievance submission to final resolution.

1
📝

Lodge Complaint

Register or log in, choose the department category, add location and optional attachment photo.

2
🏷️

Get Unique Tracking ID

Instantly receive an acknowledgment reference number (e.g. CMP-2026-XXXX).

3
🛡️

Department Review

Designated administrators review severity, assign technicians, and log real-time status updates.

4
⭐

Resolution & Feedback

Receive official closure remarks and provide satisfaction rating (1-5 stars) to ensure quality.
